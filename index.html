Documents / Desktop tool↗

md2word

Markdown copied out of ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor doesn't paste cleanly into Word, especially the math. md2word turns it into Word-ready text, a .docx or a PDF, with equations you can still edit.

Language / Browser extension↗

Russian Stress

Normal Russian text doesn't show where the stress falls. This extension adds the marks to any page, selection or text box, offline, and shows both readings for words like за́мок and замо́к.

Explore project →
Language / Study guides↗

sub2study

Turns a YouTube video into a bilingual study guide: cleaned-up captions with a timestamp on every paragraph and a vocabulary list with IPA, as a PDF and as Markdown.

Explore project →
Language / Agent skill & CLI↗

YouTube Subtitles

Paste a YouTube link, get the subtitles back as clean Markdown with timestamps, with choppy caption lines merged into paragraphs. Works as a Claude Code skill or a plain CLI.

Explore project →
Attention / Browser extension↗

AttentionFlow

Browser history shows what you opened, not what scrolled past or what you read closely. AttentionFlow tracks that on Linux DO and sums up your week, without anything leaving your browser.

Explore project →
Mac / Menu bar app↗

GesturePad

Hold the right button and draw a W to open an app, or swipe left to go back. Every gesture is editable, and the mouse wheel and trackpad can scroll in different directions.

A little about me.

I'm Dang Ding, an independent builder. I write small open-source tools, mostly for the same few annoyances: getting AI-written Markdown into Word, reading and listening in other languages, seeing where my attention goes online, and making a mouse feel right on a Mac.

If one of them breaks or you want a feature, open an issue on GitHub. PRs are welcome too.
